## Related products (for competitive reference)

- Seterra (GeoGuessr AB): map quiz app with learn and quiz modes ([Common Sense Education review](https://www.commonsense.org/education/app/seterra-geography)). Strong at quizzing, but no per-item spaced scheduling.
- Anki / AnkiDroid: general SRS with FSRS, but no native map interaction.
- Duolingo: reference for habit design and leagues; also a cautionary example of streak pressure.
